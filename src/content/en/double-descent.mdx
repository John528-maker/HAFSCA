## What is it?

**Double descent** is the claim that test error can rise near the interpolation threshold $p=n$ and then fall again as the model grows further.

## What this lab actually shows

On this polynomial engine, measured against the **true function** across many seeds, a second descent exists **geometrically** but does **not** reliably return to error competitive with the first minimum. The striking, reproducible fact is a **variance explosion** near interpolation, then **partial recovery**.

The experiment therefore does **not** promise “true double descent” as the default outcome. If a run reports partial recovery, that is the honest result.

## Why two test curves?

Primary test MSE is against $f$ (no label noise). Secondary is against noisy held-out labels. Noise adds a $\sigma^2$ pedestal to both the first and second minima and can make a ratio look closer to $1$ than the signal deserves.
