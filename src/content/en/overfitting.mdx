# What is it?

**Overfitting** is when training error keeps falling while error on unseen $x$ rises. The model is using extra capacity to memorize noise.

# Why it matters

A smaller training MSE is not a certificate of a better model. You need a check the training routine did not get to peek at — here, error against the true cubic $f$, with noisy labels shown as a second curve.

# Intuition

A cubic truth plus noise: degrees $3$–$5$ often sit on a **plateau**. The lab will **not** say “the best degree is $3$ because that is the true degree.” The data do not support a unique winner.

# Experiment

A second lab resamples $M$ training sets and fans the fitted curves. Test error in expectation is noise $+$ bias$^2$ $+$ variance. The stacked bars use divisor $M-1$ and a debiased bias$^2$; they are **not** holdout test MSE.
